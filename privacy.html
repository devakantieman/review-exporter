Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Review Exporter is a browser extension designed to export publicly accessible review information from Trustpilot pages.

Review data

Review data collected by the extension is processed and stored locally in the user's browser. Review Exporter does not operate a server to receive or store the reviews collected through the extension.

License and payment data

Payments and license management may be handled by third-party service providers. Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies. Review Exporter does not store payment card details.

Website

This website does not intentionally collect review data. Standard hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for security and delivery purposes.

User responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that their collection, storage and use of exported information complies with applicable laws, data-protection requirements and relevant website terms.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the product, website or service providers change.
